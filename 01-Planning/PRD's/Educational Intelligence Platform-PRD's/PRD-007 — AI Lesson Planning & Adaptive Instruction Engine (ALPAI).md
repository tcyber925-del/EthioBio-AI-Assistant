# Executive Summary

The AI Lesson Planning & Adaptive Instruction Engine generates classroom-ready lesson plans that adapt to actual classroom conditions.

Unlike traditional lesson planners that generate curriculum templates, EthioBio generates intelligence-driven instructional plans using:

* Readiness data
* Misconceptions
* Mastery trends
* Curriculum dependencies
* Intervention effectiveness
* Teacher preferences

Every lesson becomes personalized to the classroom.

---

# Problem Statement

Current lesson planning is disconnected from classroom reality.

Teachers typically plan using:

```text
Curriculum
+
Experience
```

while ignoring:

```text
Readiness
Misconceptions
Retention
Historical effectiveness
```

Result:

* Teaching content students are not ready for
* Repeating ineffective strategies
* Missing prerequisite gaps
* Poor intervention alignment
