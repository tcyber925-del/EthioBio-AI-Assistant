# Executive Summary

Assessment Studio is an AI-powered assessment generation, analysis, diagnostic, and intelligence platform.

Rather than merely generating quizzes, the system creates assessments specifically designed to:

* Measure mastery
* Detect misconceptions
* Evaluate readiness
* Validate interventions
* Identify prerequisite weaknesses
* Generate educational intelligence

The platform transforms assessments from grading tools into learning intelligence instruments.

---

# Problem Statement

Traditional assessments answer:

```text
Did the student get the answer right?
```

Teachers actually need:

```text
What does the student understand?

What do they misunderstand?

Why are they struggling?

Which prerequisite is missing?

Which intervention should be used?
```

Most assessment systems stop at scoring.

EthioBio should stop at understanding.
