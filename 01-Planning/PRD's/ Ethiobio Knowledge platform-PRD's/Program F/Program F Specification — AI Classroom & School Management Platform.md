# Program F Specification — AI Classroom & School Management Platform

**Program:** F

**Status:** Approved

**Priority:** Critical

# Objective

Build the AI Classroom & School Management Platform that transforms the EthioBio AI Assistant into a complete educational operating system for students, teachers, schools, and administrators.

Unlike traditional LMS platforms, this platform combines AI-powered teaching, classroom management, assessment, analytics, and administrative workflows into a unified ecosystem.

* Student learning analytics
* Teacher workspace
* Classroom AI assistants
* School administration
* Progress monitoring

* School-wide intelligence
