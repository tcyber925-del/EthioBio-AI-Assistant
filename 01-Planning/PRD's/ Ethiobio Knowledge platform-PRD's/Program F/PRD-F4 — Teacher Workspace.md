# PRD-F4 — Teacher Workspace

**Program:** F – AI Classroom & School Management Platform

**Epic:** F4

**Status:** Ready for Implementation

---

# Executive Summary

The Teacher Workspace is the primary operational hub for educators. It unifies classroom management, instructional planning, AI assistance, grading, analytics, and educational content management into a single interface.
