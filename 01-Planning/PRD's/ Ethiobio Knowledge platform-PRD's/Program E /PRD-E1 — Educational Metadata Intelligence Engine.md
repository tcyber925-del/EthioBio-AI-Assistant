# PRD-E1 — Educational Metadata Intelligence Engine

**Program:** E – Educational Intelligence Platform

**Epic:** E1

**Status:** Ready for Implementation

---

# Executive Summary

The Educational Metadata Intelligence Engine enriches Knowledge Objects with deep pedagogical understanding beyond basic metadata extraction.

It continuously analyzes educational resources and generates instructional intelligence that powers every downstream educational AI capability.
