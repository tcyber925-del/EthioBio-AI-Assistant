# Program E Specification — Educational Intelligence Platform

**Program:** E

**Status:** Approved

**Priority:** Critical

---

# Objective

Build the Educational Intelligence Platform that transforms the Knowledge Platform from a general Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system into a domain-specific AI teaching and learning assistant.

Unlike traditional AI assistants, Educational Intelligence understands pedagogy, curriculum, learning objectives, Bloom's Taxonomy, prerequisite knowledge, instructional design, and assessment.
